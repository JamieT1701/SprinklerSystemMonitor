# Device Lab — Sprinkler System Monitor

This repository preserves the engineering source, 2026 hand-built prototype, independent design competition, CAD experiments, and a **separate, unenergized heater-controller study**. The installed monitor is **supplementary only** and does **not** replace or operate any listed sprinkler, fire-alarm, dry-valve, compressor or required freeze-protection system.

> **Important navigation:** `main` is a minimal repository landing branch, **not** proof of the deployed firmware or an approved hardware schematic. The prototype documentation, public build guides, and CAD sources live on reviewed **draft branches**. Never deploy or fabricate from a draft merely because it appears in GitHub.

## How to use these records

- **Prototype assembly:** use the R6 [build guide](https://github.com/JamieT1701/SprinklerSystemMonitor/blob/docs/device-lab-2026-11-10-checkpoint/docs/BUILD-GUIDE.md), [design netlist](https://github.com/JamieT1701/SprinklerSystemMonitor/blob/docs/device-lab-2026-11-10-checkpoint/hardware/two-terminal-design.json), and owner-confirmed P4 reference. Older V1–V4 bench-guide files intentionally point to the current guide instead of preserving misleading wiring diagrams.
- **Live operations:** SFOS [Issue #880](https://github.com/JamieT1701/SFD-Intranet/issues/880) and its [facilities runbook](https://github.com/JamieT1701/SFD-Intranet/blob/platform/intranet-shared/docs/facilities/sprinkler-monitor-runbook.md) take precedence over historical “not yet live” entries in the November 10–13 engineering handoffs. Do not infer working heater controls, certified supervisory PSI or electrical verification from working cloud telemetry.
- **Hardware validation:** measure and qualify actual pressure sensor MPN, compressor/sprinkler-side pressure relationship, analog range and safe limits, P4/cable routing and enclosure clearance before claiming calibration or fabrication readiness. No live-system fault injection or air/sprinkler valve manipulation is authorized.
- **Competition source:** keep original advisor submissions isolated on their research branches; the public B1 viewer depends on the live B1 design branch. Before deleting/renaming/merging that branch, repoint and verify the viewer per its [lifecycle instructions](https://github.com/JamieT1701/SprinklerSystemMonitor/blob/design/claude-b1-engineering-20261116/research/selected-design-b1/CAD/VIEWER-LIFECYCLE.md).
- **Security and secrets:** do not commit OTA credentials, service keys, raw private employee/customer records or access tokens. Firmware and device status must be verified from the installed system; repository history alone does not establish operational state.

**Repository hygiene:** retain intentionally draft PRs until their safety/engineering gates are met. The main-branch index is only a navigation aid; it does not consolidate or merge the physical prototype, B1 commercial design, or heater experiment.
